How to explore

  • Drag the slider to tighten or loosen the access standard.
  • Click empty map to add a candidate clinic.
  • Click a clinic to remove it.
  • Hover a cell to read its population and nearest clinic.

Population grid and clinic locations are synthetic demonstration data generated in code (see README).
